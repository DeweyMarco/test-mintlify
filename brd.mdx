# Business Requirements Document

## Zoom demo

Use this section to coordinate the live Zoom walkthrough of the BRD with stakeholders.

### Meeting details

- **Platform:** Zoom
- **Meeting title:** BRD review & demo
- **Duration:** 45 minutes
- **Host:** Product team
- **Audience:** Engineering, Design, QA, and Business stakeholders

### Joining the call

1. Open the Zoom invite from your calendar.
2. Click **Join Zoom Meeting**.
3. Enter the passcode if prompted.
4. Use **Computer Audio** and enable your camera.

### Agenda

| Time | Topic | Owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 – 0:05 | Welcome & objectives | Host |
| 0:05 – 0:20 | BRD walkthrough | Product |
| 0:20 – 0:35 | Live demo | Engineering |
| 0:35 – 0:45 | Q&A and next steps | All |

### Demo checklist

- [ ] Share screen with the BRD open
- [ ] Walk through scope, goals, and success metrics
- [ ] Demo the working prototype against each requirement
- [ ] Capture feedback in the chat or shared notes
- [ ] Record the session for stakeholders who cannot attend

### After the meeting

- Share the Zoom recording and transcript with attendees.
- Log open questions and action items in the project tracker.
- Update the BRD with any agreed-upon changes before sign-off.
